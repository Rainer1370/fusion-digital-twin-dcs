# Publication Boundary

## Public in this repository

- Original layered DCS and digital-twin architecture
- Separation of supervisory, deterministic, and safety responsibilities
- Digital-twin lifecycle from simulation through advisory operations
- Need for shared naming, time, event identity, configuration, and provenance
- High-level network zoning and access principles
- General adoption and collaboration approach

## Intentionally withheld

- Facility-specific PV databases and naming inventories
- Exact device, host, address, VLAN, switch-port, or cable mappings
- Credentials, tokens, certificates, or access procedures
- Protection thresholds, trip logic, response timing, or reset procedures
- Detailed interlock cause-and-effect matrices
- Vendor selections, quantities, lead times, cost models, or procurement plans
- Exact physics-model parameters and fitting results
- Deployment scripts, automation internals, and production configuration
- Internal or non-public information belonging to any company or facility

## Release rule

Additional material should be published only when it is independently authored, based on public or authorized information, useful for technical discussion, and not sufficient by itself to expose a facility's security posture or reproduce a deployment-grade implementation.
